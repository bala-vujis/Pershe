# Style Rules — Bala's LinkedIn Posts

Reusable across all ghostwriting for Bala. Apply to every post before presenting v1.

---

## Banned phrases and patterns (AI tells — do not use)

- "In today's fast-paced world" / "In the world of X"
- "It's not just X, it's Y"
- "Game-changer" / "revolutionize" / "transform" / "unlock" (as a verb in marketing sense)
- "Delve" / "navigate the landscape" / "in the realm of"
- "Here's the thing" / "The truth is" / "Let me tell you"
- "Game plan" / "playbook" (unless literally describing a GTM playbook he wrote)
- Tricolons ("X, Y, and Z") used for rhythm — use when literally needed, not for cadence
- Em-dash-heavy rhythm. One em-dash per paragraph max. Prefer periods.
- "At the end of the day"
- "Moving the needle" / "needle-mover"
- "Supercharge" / "level up" / "10x"
- Rhetorical questions as section openers ("So what does this mean?")
- Humble-brag closers ("Hope this helps!" / "Just my two cents")
- "I've been thinking a lot about…"
- Any claim that can't be traced back to the source material

---

## Positive style rules

- Sentence fragments are fine. Encouraged, even.
- Specific numbers and tool names beat abstractions. "500 leads in 10 minutes" > "much faster."
- Name tools, people, timeframes. "n8n," "Clay," "ReachInbox," "Nick Saraev," "June 2025."
- One idea per line for the hook and the realization. Short lines breathe on mobile.
- Paragraphs max 3 lines. LinkedIn truncates hard.
- No emojis unless Bala uses them in his existing posts — default: zero emojis.
- First person. Past tense for the story. Present tense for the lesson and application.
- No "I think" / "I believe" hedging. State it.
- Defensible claims only. If a line can't be backed by the source material, cut it.

---

## Self-check before presenting v1

- [ ] Every factual claim traces back to context.md
- [ ] Zero banned phrases
- [ ] At least 4 specific named entities (tools, people, timeframes) in the first half
- [ ] Hook is 3 lines or fewer and doesn't explain itself
- [ ] The realization beat is isolated on its own short paragraph
- [ ] The application section gives a concrete 30/30/30, not generic advice
- [ ] The CTA is a question, not a link
- [ ] Word count 400–500
- [ ] Mobile-readable: no paragraph longer than 3 lines
